About Me

I'm a first-year student pursuing a BS in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Madras (2026-2030). Mathematics has fascinated me since childhood, and my love for it has only grown with time. I travelled from Delhi to the other side of the country, to Chennai, just to pursue something I truly love.

I'm especially interested in the many ways mathematics translates into real-world applications. I enjoy breaking complex problems down, looking for patterns, and asking why things work the way they do.

I'm curious by nature. I love learning new things, meeting new people, and turning ideas into something tangible. On campus, I'm involved in entrepreneurship, finance and fest organisation, and outside academics I play competitive chess, which has taught me patience, strategic thinking and resilience.
